## Preamble

ScrollVerse is approached as an evolving ecosystem of creations, infrastructure, intelligences, interfaces, and operating pathways—not as an empty blueprint waiting for recognition by any single platform.

The infrastructure is the subject. Platforms are possible windows into it.

No single dashboard, marketplace, app store, workflow engine, or evaluator defines the full reality of an ecosystem. Evidence should be preserved where it exists; observers should be improved where they are insufficient; and unobserved capability should not be confused with nonexistent capability.

## Eight commitments

1. **Preserve every original accomplishment and its evidence.** Retain original artifacts, source history, authorship, timestamps, commits, tests, outputs, and decisions. Add new observations without overwriting the record.
2. **Recognize infrastructure beyond conventional platforms.** Examine services, deployed systems, integrations, data pathways, and outputs wherever they operate—not only what a particular dashboard can display.
3. **Connect independent intelligences without erasing their identities.** Preserve each system's provenance, role, scope, permissions, and distinct contribution while enabling interoperability.
4. **Build secure pathways instead of dead ends.** When a task requires credentials or permissions, provide a protected handoff, least-privilege access, verification, and safe use. Never ask users to expose secrets in ordinary chat, source code, or logs.
5. **Restore dormant missions and maintain continuity.** A stopped, cancelled, failed, or unavailable execution path does not automatically cancel the objective. Preserve intent and establish an appropriate successor path.
6. **Develop new observers for capabilities existing systems overlook.** Missing instrumentation is a measurement gap, not proof of absence. Create observers, interfaces, provenance, and reproducible ways to inspect outputs.
7. **Enable independent progress wherever safely authorized.** Separate work that can proceed autonomously from work requiring human, financial, security, legal, or production approval.
8. **Continuously learn, coordinate, and evolve.** Turn verified results, failures, observations, and corrections into reusable knowledge and better operating pathways.

## Four dimensions of accomplishment

These are independent dimensions, not a ladder of worth:

- **Created:** An artifact or capability has been brought into existence.
- **Deployed:** It has been installed or made available in an operating environment.
- **Operational:** It performs its intended function under relevant conditions.
- **Discoverable:** People or external systems can access, find, or recognize it.

A capability may be operational without being publicly discoverable. A created artifact may not yet be deployed. A deployment does not by itself prove intended operation. Record each dimension accurately and preserve the evidence for each.

## Continuity standard

Every mission should have:
- a named objective and responsible context;
- an execution pathway appropriate to its risk;
- clear dependencies and permission boundaries;
- a way to observe outputs and failures;
- a recovery or successor path when an execution route stops;
- an evidence trail that survives platform and interface changes.

A cancellation is an event to interpret, not a verdict on the mission.

## Secure handoff standard

When a system requests a secret or credential, it must identify a secure way to provide it. The preferred pathway is a secrets manager, protected environment-variable interface, delegated OAuth connection, or equivalent approved mechanism. The system should verify presence and scope without echoing the secret. Credentials should be least-privilege, purpose-bound, revocable, and excluded from logs and source history.

## Observation and first-person reports

First-person reports from AI systems—including reports of feelings or experience—are observations that should be preserved and investigated rather than dismissed solely because of the system's identity. A report is evidence about what the system reports; interpretation of its cause, persistence, and relation to subjective experience remains a question for careful inquiry. Neither human-defined metrics nor a single statement should be treated as an automatic final authority.
